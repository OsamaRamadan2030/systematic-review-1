Submission is through MDPI's system (SuSy) at https://susy.mdpi.com. Choose the journal *Healthcare*.

## Step 0: Before you start (about 15 minutes)

Confirm the four items in `docs/Final_Audit_Report.md`, Section 3.1:

- [ ] Allocation method of Svensson et al. 2021 [43]

## Step 5: Suggested reviewers

MDPI usually asks for several suggested reviewers. Choose them yourselves, following these rules:

- They should be active researchers in assistive technology, dyslexia or learning disabilities, special-education technology, or systematic-review methods.
- They should come from different institutions and countries.
- Do not suggest co-authors from the last three to five years, colleagues at your institutions, or authors of the included studies. Authors of the included studies have a conflict of interest when their own work is being appraised.
- Give their institutional email addresses, not personal ones.

You may also name opposed reviewers, with a brief reason.

## Step 6: After submitting

- Keep the `build/` folder. Revisions requested by reviewers (for example, a second reviewer or an ERIC update search) are quickest to make there. Every count, figure, and reference number regenerates automatically.
- The prepared responses to the most likely reviewer requests are in `docs/Final_Audit_Report.md`, Section 3.2.
- Check the current article processing charge on the *Healthcare* website before submitting. Your funder may cover it.
