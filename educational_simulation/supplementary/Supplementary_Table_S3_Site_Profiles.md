# Supplementary Table S3. Site profiles: mobilisation guidance, professional responsibilities, referral arrangements and service availability

> **Simulation notice.** These site profiles belong to an educational simulation. Hospitals, wards, documents and staffing figures are invented for teaching.

Profiles were compiled in February–March 2026, before interviewing began, from three sources: hospital policies and guidelines; site-description forms completed with ward nurse managers and physiotherapy leads; and recruitment-visit field notes (Section 2.2). Where written procedures and staff reports differed, both are shown and marked *Written* and *Reported*. The profiles describe arrangements during the study period. They do not show that these arrangements were followed in every encounter, or that they applied to earlier episodes recalled by professionals.
