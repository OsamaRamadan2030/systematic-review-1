## Major issues found and fixed
1. **Sample gender.** "16 women and two men" was wrong; all participants were women. Fixed in the abstract, 2.3, 3.1, Table 1 and its note, member-reflection sampling (2.8), the limitations, and COREQ 12.
2. **Interviewer.** "The male lead researcher… He was…" was wrong. Two female researchers conducted the interviews. Fixed in 2.5, 2.9, the limitations and COREQ 1–8.
3. **Theoretical circularity** (the main reason for rejection). Moral distress is now a sensitising lens only. Collective theories are applied explicitly at the interpretive stage, rival readings were tested, and moral negotiation is framed as provisional.
